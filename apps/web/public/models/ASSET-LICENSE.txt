Frost Crab, Christmas Pine and the pixel textures are original procedural
assets from the earlier Snowbound / Winter Arc project, distributed under
the project's MIT license. No Mojang/Minecraft textures are included.

The supplied Repomix XML omits binary assets. This ZIP restores the default
PNG/GLB files from the earlier project. Replace them with your newer custom
assets if you changed the binary files since that version.

Replacement assets retain their own licensing and attribution requirements.
